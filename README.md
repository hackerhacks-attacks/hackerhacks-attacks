## Review Standard

Public material is expected to be:

- clear
- ethical
- professionally written
- evidence-based
- safe for review
- limited to authorized contexts
- free from unnecessary personal disclosure

---

## Professional Boundaries

- Public material avoids sensitive targets, private data, and internal operational details.
- Claims are limited to what is visible or linked publicly.
- Personal identity is minimized; reviewable technical output is prioritized.
- Public examples are for review only and are not a grant of reuse rights.
